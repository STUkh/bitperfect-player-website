Materials

Lacquer

The site's glossy surface: a diagonal wash from the upper left, a 1px specular rim, a darker lower lip and a soft sheen. Every card, plate and control takes it from lacquer.css. Use Card (or the lacquer class), never a flat glass box.

PlayCta

Every install button is this one: a Button that goes to the Play listing and carries the Google Play mark. Gold by default; secondary or outline when another action already holds the gold.

StoreBadges

Graphite lacquer badges with the store mark and two lines of text, for the header and footer. sm header, md default, lg 220px minimum, icon 44x44 below 640px.

PlayStoreMark

The one Google Play glyph, in currentColor: dark on the gold key-cap, white on a graphite badge.

The control rules

  1. Buttons never move on hover or press. Only the face, the sheen and the rim light up.
  2. No arrow or chevron inside a button or a store badge. Text links and PagerLink may carry one.
  3. Gold fills are only the install key-cap and the Founder plan. Every other button, badge and chip is graphite lacquer.
  4. Every install button is PlayCta. Never a play triangle, never a hand-made gold button.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 5, 2026

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes what the Bitperfect Player app ("the App") collects, why it collects it, and what control you have over your data.

1. Summary (non-binding: the full sections below govern)

  • The App is a local/network music player. Your music library, playback history, and settings stay on your device.
  • The on-device AI audio enhancer processes audio entirely on your device. No audio is ever uploaded, streamed, or transmitted anywhere for enhancement.

4. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction) and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
