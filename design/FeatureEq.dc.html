32-band parametric EQ

Shape an exact compensation curve with up to 32 bands, seven filter shapes, and Q that runs from broad warmth to a pinpoint notch. Then bypass it in one tap to hear the file untouched.

Bands 1 to 32

Bands

Thirty-two bands, each one fully independent

Place a filter anywhere between 20 Hz and 20 kHz and move it by up to 24 dB in either direction. Nothing is snapped to a preset slider position, so a correction curve from a measurement rig transfers over exactly as it was measured.

Span 20 Hz to 20 kHz
Gain -24.0 dB to +24.0 dB
Filter shapes

Seven topologies, not just a bell

A bell handles most corrections. The other six are there for the cases it cannot: shelving the whole top end, rolling off subsonic rumble before it reaches the driver, or notching out one resonance without touching its neighbours.

Peak Low shelf High shelf Low pass High pass All pass Notch
Q factor

From broad warmth to a surgical notch

Q is continuous from 0.10 to 10.0, not stepped. At the wide end a single band tilts a whole region gently. At the narrow end it removes one peak and leaves the octave either side of it alone.

Same gain, different Q. Q 0.7 spreads across two octaves. Q 8.0 sits on one frequency.

Bypass

One tap back to the untouched file

Bypass is a hard bypass. The EQ leaves the signal path entirely and the badge returns to Bit-perfect, so you can A and B your curve against the original without wondering whether something is still running.

While bypassed the engine is bit-exact again, and the indicator says so.
