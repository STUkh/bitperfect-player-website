Questions

The ones people actually ask

What does bit-perfect actually mean here?

The samples reaching your DAC are numerically identical to the ones in the file. Where direct hardware access is not achievable, the app shows the real state instead of claiming otherwise.

Do I need an external DAC?

No. The phone's own output works too, but it goes through the system mixer, so the bit-perfect badge is reserved for direct USB output to an external DAC.

What audio formats does it play?

Fifteen. FLAC, ALAC, WAV, AIFF and DSD (DSF and DFF, via DoP or native), plus DSD disc images, Monkey's Audio, WavPack, True Audio, TAK, Musepack, Opus, Ogg Vorbis, MP3 and AAC. If you have an archive full of the formats other players refuse, it will most likely open it.

Can I turn off the AI processing?

Yes, and it is off by default. Turning it on moves the badge to Enhanced rather than Bit-perfect, because the samples have changed.

Will it work on my phone?

Android 8.0 and newer. Every feature runs on any phone that meets that, except the AI Enhancer, which needs a phone with a neural chip. The app checks for one before offering it.

What does the app send over the internet?

Four things, and no more. Crash reports and album artwork lookups, both on by default and switchable in Settings, the one-time model download when you turn on an AI feature, and the check that says your subscription is active. No analytics, no ads, no account. Your files and your listening never leave the phone.

What is in the DSP rack?

A 32-band parametric EQ with seven filter types, headphone crossfeed in three strengths, ReplayGain read from your file tags, and a true-peak limiter at a -0.6 dBTP ceiling. The whole rack switches off for bit-perfect output.

What does it cost?

$35 for 12 months, with the first 7 days free for new customers. One subscription covers the whole app, there is no higher tier, and nothing is switched off to sell you one. Billed through Google Play and cancellable there at any time.

Do the AI features work offline?

Yes. A model downloads once, the first time you enable the feature. Everything after that runs on the device with no network call.
