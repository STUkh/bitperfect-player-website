## 2. Executive Summary

A full end-to-end audit was conducted across all 17 routes of the Bitperfect Player website using automated Playwright headless Chrome testing at 1440×900 (desktop) and 390×844 (mobile), accompanied by 5 specialized parallel audit subagents.

### Key Constraints Enforced:

1. **Calm, Minimal, Low Cognitive Load:** Eliminated visual noise and SaaS tropes across catalog, FAQ, changelog, and feature pages.
2. **NO Chips / Pill Badges:** Removed `rounded-full` category filter badges from `/features/catalog` and `/faq`, status pill badges from `/changelog`, and boxed tag clusters from feature detail beats.
3. **NO Decorative Numbering:** Removed `Q01`..`Q09` accordion numbering from `/faq`, circular badge numbers `01`..`03` and card prefix numbers `01 ·`..`04 ·` from `/faq/why-npu-ai-enhancer-not-working`, and pipeline numbering `01`..`07` from `/features/usb-dac`.
4. **NO Box-in-Box:** Preserved strict 1px hairlines and vertical whitespace rather than nested borders and cards.
5. **Centered Editorial Flow:** Maintained focused, single-column reading containers (`max-w-[760px]` / `max-w-[840px]`) for changelog and FAQ.
6. **Stationary Buttons:** Confirmed stationary baselines across all interactive buttons (`transform: none` on hover/active, diagonal specular sheen sweep only).
7. **Strict Typographic Bans:** Eliminated all em-dashes (`—`) and en-dashes (`–`) from visitor-facing copy per DESIGN.md line 516.
8. **Fact Inviolability:** Verified exact preservation of 768 kHz, DSD512, 85/25 dyadic volume steps, LMS/Roon 24/192, DCA (zero DTS), Lenovo forum URL, SDK GitHub URL, Founder rate ($19.95/yr until May 31, 2027), and Standard rate ($29.95/yr).

## 3. Findings by Audit Subagent

### 1. Impeccable Design Auditor

- **Horizontal Overflow:** 0 overflow issues across all 16 routes at both 1440px and 390px.
- **Button Hover Lift:** Pagers on `/features/[slug]` used `translateY(-1px)`, violating the stationary baseline rule.
- **Leftover Badges / Chips (P1):** Flagged `rounded-full` filter pills on `/features/catalog` and `/faq`, release status pills on `/changelog`, and `beat.chips` boxed clusters on `/features/[slug]`.
- **Decorative Numbering (P1):** Flagged `Q01..Q09` on `/faq`, `01..03` and `01·..04·` on `/faq/why-npu-ai-enhancer-not-working`, and `01..07` on `/features/usb-dac`.
- **Touch Targets (P1):** Breadcrumb back links (19px) and footer navigation links (36px) fell below the 44px mobile touch target minimum.

### 2. Copy Editor

- **Banned Typography (P1):** Found em-dashes `—` in `src/PRIVACY_POLICY.md` (lines 164-165) and `src/pages/faq/why-npu-ai-enhancer-not-working.astro` (lines 34, 100), plus en-dashes `–` in `src/data/faq.ts` (lines 128, 180) and `src/data/technical-features.ts` (line 263).
- **Tone & Marketing Hyperbole (P1):** Flagged salesy copy: "surgical control", "LIMITED FOUNDER OFFER", "STANDARD ANNUAL PLAN", "Hear what your music actually sounds like", "watch the badge go bit-perfect", "patent-grade precision", "The ones people actually ask", and inaccurate color references ("emerald", "violet").
- **Fact Verification:** 100% verified all 9 required facts. Fixed typo "Rust Symphony engine" to "Rust Symphonia engine".

### 3. Structure & Anomaly Researcher

- **Broken 404 Route (P0):** `src/pages/404.astro` rendered the obsolete `HoldingPage.astro` claiming the site was parked/offline. Proper 404 was parked in `src/pages/_404.astro`.
- **Duplicate Route Leak (P0):** `src/pages/preview-redesign.astro` was an exact clone of `index.astro` being built to `/preview-redesign/index.html`.
- **Broken FAQ Anchor Links (P0):** `<details>` in `src/pages/faq/index.astro` lacked `id={item.id}`, and footer links in `src/data/navigation.ts` pointed to non-existent question fragments.
- **Dead Underscore Files (P0):** `src/pages/_index.astro`, `src/pages/_features/`, `src/components/HoldingPage.astro`.
- **Discrepancy (P1):** Network receiver casting ceiling in `src/data/features.ts` (384 kHz) contradicted `technical-features.ts` and the LMS/Roon 24/192 spec.

### 4. Best-Solution Evaluator (Designer)

- **Catalog:** Recommended replacing 7 `rounded-full` pill buttons with calm editorial text tabs and underlines.
- **Changelog:** Recommended replacing version badge pills with quiet mono metadata text, and unifying column widths to 760px.
- **Features Teaser:** Recommended removing nested "Explore feature ->" chevrons with `translate-x` inside cards so the whole card acts as a clean plate.
- **Features Detail:** Recommended removing `01..07` numbering from usb-dac Signal Path and unboxing `beat.chips` into typographic tokens separated by middots `·`.

### 5. Architect

- **Token Hygiene (P1):** Flagged non-deterministic `Math.random()` in `PhoneFrame.astro` creating unpredictable SVG IDs on every build.
- **Dead Classes & CSS (P1):** Unused `.cta-action` class passed into `Button.astro`, dead `.section-divider`, `@utility badge`, and `@utility z-1` in `global.css`.
- **Touch Targets:** Sticky TOC in `LegalDocument.astro` had 36px links.
